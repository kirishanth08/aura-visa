New Zealand Green List Fast-Track: 2026 Tier 1 Straight to Residence Roles

Immigration New Zealand (INZ) has expanded its Tier 1 Green List occupations, allowing qualified international doctors, engineers, ICT executives, and construction leaders to apply for direct residency upon arrival.

Aroha Te Rangi, LIA
Licensed Immigration Adviser • IAA License #20190142 (Auckland)

1. Overview of Tier 1 Straight to Residence (STR)

Unlike the points-tested Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) which requires 6 points across qualifications, registration, and domestic work, the Green List Tier 1 pathway provides direct-to-residence capability without a waiting period.

Applicants need an offer of full-time employment (minimum 30 hours/week) from an Accredited Employer in New Zealand. Provided the employment meets the specified ANZSCO specification and the role's median wage standard ($31.61 NZD/hour, or 1.5x median for selected ICT roles), candidates can lodge their resident visa before ever arriving in the country.

2. Essential Tier 1 Occupation Disciplines

Engineering & Infrastructure

Structural, geotechnical, chemical, and electrical engineers with Washington Accord accredited degrees and Engineering New Zealand recognition.

Healthcare & Specialist Medicine

General practitioners, surgeons, registered nurses, clinical psychologists, and midwives holding statutory Medical Council of New Zealand registration.

Spouses of Tier 1 Green List applicants are automatically eligible for open work visas with full employment flexibility across the New Zealand economy.
