Common Questions

Consultation Procedures & Confidentiality

What happens after I submit this consultation request?

An intake coordinator reviews your details within 4 business hours, confirms counsel availability, and sends calendar invites with a preliminary checklist of documents to have handy.

Is the initial consultation completely confidential?

Yes. All consultations are governed by strict bar association regulations and legal professional confidentiality. Your information is never shared with third parties or employers.

Can I bring my spouse or business partner into the video session?

Absolutely. We actively encourage family co-applicants or business co-founders to join the meeting so all pertinent factors (spousal education, language bands, asset ownership) can be addressed.
