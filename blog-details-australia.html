Australia Subclass 189/190 GSM Invitation Round Analysis & Cutoff Forecasts

Department of Home Affairs issued 8,500 SkillSelect invitations in the latest allocation window. Here is our granular analysis of minimum point requirements across engineering, ICT, and allied health occupations.

Liam O'Connor, RMA
Registered Migration Agent • MARA #1804219 (Sydney)

1. Executive Summary of the Latest SkillSelect Round

The Australian Government has published official statistical outcomes for the targeted SkillSelect round. In an aggressive push to mitigate critical national skills deficits, Home Affairs allocated over 65% of invitations to candidates residing onshore with valid Australian work experience, while offshore engineers and registered nurses captured the remaining allocations.

Notably, the minimum score threshold for civil, mechanical, and electrical engineers dropped to 80 points, down from 85 in the previous quarter. Software engineers and ICT business analysts, however, continue to face competitive cutoffs of 85 to 90 points.

2. State Nomination Allocations (Subclass 190 & 491)

New South Wales (NSW)

Prioritizing candidates with active employment in Greater Sydney. Minimum onshore tenure: 6 months in target ANZSCO code.

Victoria (VIC)

Strong focus on clean energy, advanced manufacturing, and health sciences. Over 3,200 nomination slots remaining for 2026.

Candidates should ensure their Skills Assessment from Engineers Australia or ACS remains valid for at least 60 days post-EOI submission to prevent invalidation during statutory verification.
