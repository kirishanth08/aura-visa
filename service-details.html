Selection Formula

How the CRS Point System Evaluates Candidates

The Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) awards points out of 1,200 based on core human capital, spousal factors, and government provincial nominations.

Max 110 Points
Age Factor

Maximum points are awarded to applicants aged between 20 and 29 years, tapering down after age 30.

Max 150 Points
Education Level

Verified via Educational Credential Assessment (ECA). Master's and Ph.D. degrees earn highest bracket.

Max 160 Points
Language (IELTS / CELPIP)

Achieving Canadian Language Benchmark (CLB 9 / IELTS 8777) delivers an exponential score surge.

Bonus +600 Points
Provincial Nomination

Securing a nomination through Ontario, BC, or Alberta PNP guarantees an immediate Invitation to Apply (ITA).

Verification Standards

Mandatory Document Dossier

A single missing stamp or miscalculated work tenure can trigger an immediate application rejection. Our legal audit team verifies every entry against IRCC guidelines.

ECA Report (WES / ICAS)
Equivalency report demonstrating degrees match Canadian educational standards.
Language Test Results (IELTS / TEF)
Official score report less than 2 years old verifying English and/or French fluency.
Employment Reference Letters on Letterhead
Detailed reference letters mapping duties directly to Canadian NOC 2021 codes.
Police Clearance & Upfront Medicals
Certificates from all nations resided in for 6+ consecutive months, plus panel physician exams.

Proof of Settlement Funds (2026)

IRCC mandates unencumbered liquid funds depending on the number of family members migrating:

Family Size Required CAD ($) USD Equivalent
1 (Single Applicant) $14,690 CAD ~$10,800 USD
2 (Couple) $18,288 CAD ~$13,500 USD
3 (Couple + 1 Child) $22,483 CAD ~$16,600 USD
4 (Family of Four) $27,297 CAD ~$20,150 USD
Funds must be maintained in accessible liquid bank accounts (fixed deposits, savings).
