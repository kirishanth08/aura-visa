Procedural Law

5-Stage Statutory Filing Lifecycle

Our procedural workflow follows strict statutory compliance as regulated by the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act (IRPA) and Australian Migration Act 1958.

Milestone 01
Fiduciary Retainer

Statutory conflicts check, formal retainer agreement signing, and escrow trust account funding.

Milestone 02
ECA & Biometrics

Official academic credential assessment (WES), police clearances, and medical examination filings.

Milestone 03
Portal Lodgement

Authorized government portal lodgement with formal Notice of Representation (IMM 5476 / Form 956).

Milestone 04
Case Adjudication

Case officer interaction, Request for Further Information (RFI) handling, and biometric validation.

Milestone 05
COPR & Stamp

Confirmation of Permanent Residence (COPR) issuance, visa passport vignette stamping, and port-of-entry dispatch.
