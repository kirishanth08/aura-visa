US EB-2 NIW: Self-Petition Green Cards for AI Specialists & Tech Pioneers

How machine learning researchers, cybersecurity architects, and startup founders can waive the PERM labor certification requirement under the precedent decision *Matter of Dhanasar* to secure lawful permanent residency.

Marcus Thorne, Esq.
US Immigration Partner • New York State Bar & AILA Member

1. The Power of Self-Petitioning Under Dhanasar

Traditional employment-based immigrant visas in the United States require a permanent job offer from an employer and a grueling 18-to-24 month labor market test via the Department of Labor's PERM process. The National Interest Waiver (NIW), established under INA § 203(b)(2)(B), allows the candidate to completely bypass employer sponsorship and petition on their own behalf.

Under USCIS Policy Manual guidance aligning with presidential executive directives on Artificial Intelligence, advanced degree professionals working in dual-use technologies, robotics, clean energy, and quantum computing are granted favorable administrative adjudications.

2. The Three-Prong Dhanasar Framework

Prong 1: Substantial Merit

The endeavor must hold transformative national or global implications in science, commerce, healthcare, or national defense.

Prong 2: Well Positioned

Demonstrated by citations, peer-reviewed publications, venture capital backing, proprietary patents, and expert letters of recommendation.

Prong 3: Balance of Benefits

Proving the United States benefits significantly more from waiving the job offer requirement than enforcing it.

USCIS Premium Processing (Form I-907) is now fully operational for all Form I-140 NIW petitions, guaranteeing an adjudicated decision within 45 calendar days.
