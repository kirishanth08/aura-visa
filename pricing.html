Pay as You Progress Through Each Government Stage

We never demand full legal fees upfront. Retainers are divided across logical milestones tied to tangible results in your immigration journey.

Stage 1: Retainer Signing (30%)
Commences credential assessment, language benchmarking, and portfolio preparation.
Stage 2: Official Pool Entry (35%)
Paid when your profile is officially submitted and verified in the government pool.
Stage 3: Invitation to Apply & Final Filing (35%)
Due only when your government invitation (ITA) is secured and e-APR is prepared.
