Germany Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte): Point System & Job Search Guidelines

The Federal Republic of Germany has inaugurated the Chancenkarte points matrix under Section 20a of the Residence Act (*AufenthG*). Discover how non-EU professionals can obtain a 12-month work-search visa without a prior employment contract.

Hans-Dietrich Becker, RA
Rechtsanwalt & Immigration Counsel • Frankfurt Bar Association

1. Statutory Architecture of the Chancenkarte

Historically, entering Germany for economic employment required either a concrete binding contract qualifying for the EU Blue Card or a lengthy, bureaucratic vocational equivalency determination (*Anerkennungsverfahren*).

The Opportunity Card abolishes this bottleneck. Candidates who possess a state-recognized university degree or vocational certificate (minimum 2 years training) and either German language ability (CEFR A1) or English ability (CEFR B2) can enter the points pool. Candidates attaining at least 6 out of 14 points are granted a 1-year residence permit with part-time work rights (up to 20 hours/week) and 2-week probationary employment trials with prospective German employers.

2. Core Points Categories

Partial Recognition (4 Points)

Awarded if the Central Office for Foreign Education (ZAB) certifies your foreign diploma has substantial equivalence to a German reference qualification.

Shortage Occupation (1 Point)

Applicable to engineers, IT architects, physicians, pharmacists, and STEM academics facing acute national labor shortages.

Financial self-sufficiency must be demonstrated through a blocked account (*Sperrkonto*) containing €12,324 (€1,027 per month) or a binding declaration of commitment (*Verpflichtungserklärung*) from an enterprise sponsor in Germany.

Eligibility Scoring

Chancenkarte Points Scoring Breakdown

Statutory Criterion Legal Requirement Awarded Points
Partial Equivalence Recognition ZAB / Chamber of Commerce formal notification 4 Points
Professional Work Experience 5 years experience in field within last 7 years 3 Points
German Language Competency CEFR B1 Goethe / TestDaF certificate 2 Points
Candidate Age Bracket Age 35 or younger on date of application 2 Points
Previous Stay in Germany Verified legal residency of at least 6 continuous months 1 Point
