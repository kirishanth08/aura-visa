Navigating UK Skilled Worker Minimum Salary Rules & Immigration Salary List

A comprehensive statutory briefing on the UK Home Office's £38,700 general baseline, allowable discount tradeable points, and strategic routes for Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS) allocation.

Victoria Stirling, LLB
Head of UK & Corporate Practice • Law Society of England and Wales (#610482)

1. The £38,700 Baseline & The Going Rate Rule

Following the Home Office's overhaul of Appendix Skilled Worker, employers must guarantee sponsored workers a minimum gross annual remuneration matching whichever is higher: the revised general threshold of £38,700 or the median "going rate" for the specific Standard Occupational Classification (SOC) code.

For high-value corporate transfers and technology hires, this creates substantial budget requirements. However, multiple statutory exemptions and discount mechanisms remain available under Tradeable Points Option B, C, D, and E.

2. Tradeable Points Discount Pathways

Relevant STEM PhD

Reduces minimum threshold by 20% down to £30,960, provided the academic doctoral qualification is verified via Ecctis as directly relevant to the role.

Immigration Salary List (ISL)

Roles designated by MAC as critical national shortage allow sponsorship at £30,960 or the role's discounted going rate.

New entrants to the UK labor market (graduates under age 26, Tier 4 / Student visa switchers, and those working toward chartered professional status) also benefit from a 30% reduction, lowering the statutory minimum to £27,090 for up to 3 cumulative years.
