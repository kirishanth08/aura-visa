Our Journey

From a Toronto Boutique Law Firm to an International Authority

In 2011, AuraVisa began with a single mission: to eliminate the ambiguity, predatory fees, and misrepresentations that too often plague international migration. Our founders, seasoned Canadian and British solicitors, created a boutique practice anchored in fiduciary ethics.

Today, our firm represents clients across 45 countries. We have shepherded software engineers, medical doctors, academic researchers, and whole families through complex Express Entry draws, Australian SkillSelect pools, and UK sponsor licenses with uncompromising attention to statutory detail.
